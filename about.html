About Me

Engineering by day, welding by evening

Hi, I'm Ed — I'm studying Mechanical and Electrical Engineering at the University of Bath, with two years left before I head into my third year this September. My late Grandad taught me to weld, and I've loved doing it ever since. I'm now using what he taught me to help fund the rest of my time at university.

Outside the workshop I'm a competitive track cyclist, racing at international level with the goal of being selected for Team GB and competing at the LA Olympics.

What I offer

Everything I make is welded by hand in my spare time, around studying and training — nothing is machine-perfect, and that's the point. The Products page shows what's currently available to order. If you're after something that isn't listed but could still be welded — anything from garden furniture to fences — send me an email and let's talk about it.
